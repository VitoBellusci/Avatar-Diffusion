I Tiny Text-Conditioned Avatar Diffusion from Scratch
1 Task Overview
In this assignment, you will build a small text-conditioned diffusion model that generates cartoon avatars from short structured descriptions. The goal is not to call a ready-made text-to-image system: you must implement and train the main model from scratch on a small image dataset. The final result should be both technically inspectable and easy to demonstrate. A user should be able to enter a prompt such as "a blue cartoon avatar with round eyes and exaggerated proportions", choose a seed, and inspect the generated 32x32 or 64x64 image.
Figure 1: Illustrative target space for the assignment: a small set of cartoon avatars with different text-controllable attributes such as hair, eyes, accessories, proportions, and color accents. The research question is:
Can a very small diffusion model, whose denoiser and text encoder are both trained from scratch, generalize to combinations of avatar attributes that were not observed together during training?
2 Objective

· a reproducible image and caption preprocessing pipeline; · a text tokenizer and text encoder trained from scratch; · a compact U-Net denoiser trained from scratch;

3 Dataset
The recommended dataset is the Google Cartoon Set, which contains labelled 2D cartoon avatars in 10k and 100k versions. See the official page: Google Cartoon Set. The dataset attributes should be converted into deterministic, multi-attribute captions. For example:
"a boy with blue colors, round eyes, and exaggerated proportions"
The caption vocabulary and tokenizer must be derived from the training split only. A 10k subset is an acceptable fallback; the 100k version is recommended for the extended configuration.
4 Data Preprocessing
All preprocessing steps should be clearly documented and justified in your report. Resize the avatars to the selected resolution, normalize the image values consistently, and generate deterministic multi-attribute captions from the dataset metadata. The tokenizer vocabulary must be constructed from the training split only. Do not use validation or test captions, normalization statistics, or attribute frequencies when constructing the training representation. Save the split definition and preprocessing configuration so that the complete pipeline can be reproduced.
Compositional Split The main split must be defined over attribute combinations rather than only over individual images. Hold out a controlled set of combinations, for example a particular color­artwork or color­proportion combination, and ensure that the corresponding combination is absent from training. You must report:
· which individual attributes appear in training; · which combinations are held out; · the number of train, validation, ordinary test, and compositional-OOD examples; · how the caption vocabulary is constructed without using validation or test text.
Mandatory From-Scratch Constraints The following components are forbidden in the main solution:
· Stable Diffusion, Tiny-SD, SDXL, Flux, or any other pretrained diffusion checkpoint; · CLIP, T5, BERT, or any pretrained text encoder used for conditioning;

5 Model Architecture
The recommended minimum model is a pixel-space DDPM with a compact U-Net. A reasonable envelope is:
· base channels: 64-128; · two or three spatial resolutions; · residual blocks with time embeddings; · a 2-4 layer Transformer text encoder trained from scratch; · text hidden size: 64-128; · conditioning injected through cross-attention or other techniques.
